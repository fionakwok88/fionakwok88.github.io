Mission

Our research seeks to answer fundamental questions in geomechanics and geomaterial behavior through computational methods and engineering applications. We study complex geophysical flows, sustainable ground materials, and energy related geomechanical processes, connecting particle-scale mechanisms with challenges in infrastructure, hazard resilience, and environmental sustainability.

By combining soil and rock mechanics with numerical techniques including DEM, CFD, and LBM, this work aims to support safer engineering and more sustainable use of the subsurface.
