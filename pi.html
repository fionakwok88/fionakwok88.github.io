Dr. Fiona C.Y. Kwok 郭頌怡博士

BEng(Hons), MEng, PhD (Cantab), PE (California), MHKIE

Dr. Fiona C.Y. Kwok is a geomechanics researcher whose work spans geohazards, sustainable geomaterials, energy geomechanics, and underground space. Her research combines fundamental soil and rock mechanics with computational methods to understand complex geomaterial behavior and address engineering challenges.

She was an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at The University of Hong Kong from 2010 to 2025. She welcomes motivated prospective Master’s, PhD, and postdoctoral researchers with a strong foundation in soil or rock mechanics.
