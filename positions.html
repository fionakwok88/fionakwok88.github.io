Research Positions

Highly motivated and intellectually curious candidates are welcome to enquire about potential Master’s, PhD, and postdoctoral opportunities.

Candidate Profile

  • Solid background in fundamental soil or rock mechanics
  • Genuine interest in innovative numerical techniques for geomechanics
  • Independent, hardworking, creative, and collaborative approach to research
  • Commitment to producing high-quality research and publishing in strong journals and conferences

Enquiries

Please email a brief introduction, a summary of research interests, and CV to fionkwok@cityu.edu.hk.
